💬 Ab WhatsApp Par Confirm Karein

Request bhejne ke baad WhatsApp wala button ayega — ek tap mein message gym ko chala jayega. Aapki maloomat kahin aur share nahi hoti.

Trial mein kya hota hai?

Floor ka round, coach se goal par baat, aur halki si training agar aap chahein. Jute aur towel saath le aayein — paani ki bottle bhool jayein to desk se mil jayegi.

Kya mujhe pehle call karni chahiye?

Form kaafi hai, lekin jaldi ho to seedha call ya WhatsApp kar lein: 0321 7593916. Shaam 5–8 desk masroof rehta hai, jawab mein thori der ho sakti hai.

Gari / bike kahan khari karoon?

Building ke aas-paas street parking hai. Bike ke liye neeche jagah mil jati hai — phir lift se seedha 2nd floor.
