The Club · Est. in Y-Block DHA

Ye gym shor ke liye nahi, kaam ke liye bana hai.

Power House ne ek seedhi si baat samjhi: DHA mein gyms bohat hain, lekin jahan jagah, saaf mahol aur coach ki tawajjo — teeno ek saath milein, woh jagah kam hai. Yehi khali jagah bharne ke liye ye floor banaya gaya.

Shaam ka floor — lights kam, focus zyada
01 — Jagah

Y-Block ki sab se bari floor.

Building #179 ki doosri manzil ka poora floor training ke liye hai. Is ka matlab seedha hai: bench ke liye line nahi, dumbbell ke liye dhakka-mukki nahi, aur deadlift ke liye kona dhoondna nahi parta.

  • Free-weight zone — dumbbells, barbells, plates har range mein
  • Machine line — chest, back, legs, shoulders alag stations
  • Cardio line — treadmill, cycling, cross-trainer
  • Classes ke liye alag hissa — awaz aur jagah dono ka khayal
02 — Mahol

Safai, tameez, aur sakoon se training.

/ A

Ladies ka alag section

Separate training area, alag classes aur lockers. Bohat si members sirf isi wajah se yahan rukti hain — mahol mehfooz, coach friendly.

/ B

Machines ki dekh-bhaal

Cable phansi ho ya bench dheela — floor par hi theek hota hai. Equipment saaf aur lubricated rakhna roz ka kaam hai, kabhi-kabhar ka nahi.

/ C

Har level welcome

Pehla din ho ya competition ki tayyari — yahan koi kisi ko judge nahi karta. Coach beginner ko utni hi izzat se sikhata hai jitni advanced ko.

03 — Tarika-e-kaar

Teen usool, roz ke.

Usool 01

Form pehle

Weight baad mein barhega, pehle movement sahi hoga. Galat form par coach wahin rokta hai — ye nakhra nahi, hifazat hai.

Usool 02

Plan kagaz par

Har member ka goal likha jata hai — wazan, taqat ya stamina. Phir har haftay hisab hota hai: kya barha, kya ruka, kya badalna hai.

Usool 03

Khana bhi training hai

Sirf paseena kaafi nahi. Nutrition guidance har membership ke saath hai — desi khane ke hisab se, bina ajeeb diet charts ke.

Khud aa kar dekhein

Tasveeron se zyada mahol bolta hai.

Ek trial session book karein — floor ka round lagega, coach milega, phir faisla aapka.
