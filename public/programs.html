Programs · Har goal ke liye

Training jo samajh mein aye, asar bhi kare.

Chaar bare raste hain — taqat, wazan, classes, ya one-to-one coaching. Har raste ki shuruaat free assessment se hoti hai, taake plan aapke jism ke hisab se bane, kisi chart ke hisab se nahi.

Strength zone — barbell pehle
Program 01

Strength & Muscle

Jism banana ho to barbell se dosti karni parti hai. Squat, bench, deadlift, rows — buniyadi lifts, sahi form, aur har haftay thora sa zyada weight. Yehi pura formula hai.

  • 4–5 din ka split — push / pull / legs ya upper / lower
  • Har lift par coach ki form-check, khaas kar naye members ke liye
  • Mahine ke aakhir mein strength test — number bolta hai, andaza nahi

Kiske liye? Muscle gain, taqat, ya woh log jo pehli dafa sahi tareeqay se seekhna chahte hain.

Cardio zone — dil mazboot, wazan halka
Program 02

Fat Loss & Cardio

Wazan girana koi magic nahi — khana theek, neend theek, aur roz thora paseena. Cardio zone ke saath circuit training aur nutrition guidance — teeno mil kar kaam karte hain.

  • Treadmill, cycling, cross-trainer — dil ke hisab se pace set
  • Haftay mein 2 circuit sessions — poora jism, kam waqt
  • Desi khane par mabni nutrition plan — roti band nahi, hisab theek

Kiske liye? Weight loss, stamina, ya BP / sugar ke saath jeene walay — doctor ki ijazat ke saath.

Program 03

Kickboxing, Zumba, Aerobics — group wala scene.

Akele bore ho jate hain? Group class mein music, dost aur coach ki awaz — waqt guzarne ka pata hi nahi chalta, aur calories bhi udaas ho kar nikalti hain.

Class A

Kickboxing

Bag work, jabs, hooks, kicks — gussa bhi nikalta hai, ghutne bhi mazboot hote hain. Gloves desk se mil jate hain.

  • Mon / Wed / Fri — 6:30 PM
  • Beginner batch alag
Class B

Zumba & Aerobics

Rhythm ke saath full-body movement. Ladies batches dopahar ko — mahol friendly, coach female members ke saath.

  • Tue / Thu — 6:00 PM + ladies batch
  • Har fitness level ke liye
Class C

Cycling & Circuit

Saturday wala full-body circuit — stations badalte rehte hain, paseena rukta nahi. Haftay ka behtareen ikhtitam.

  • Saturday — 5:30 PM
  • Cycling roz cardio zone mein
