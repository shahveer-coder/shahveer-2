Our Story

A cozy cafe in the heart of Islamabad

Cafe Sierra, located at Plot 7, Madina Market, F-8/2, has become a go-to spot for coffee lovers, steak fans and friends looking for a relaxed dine-out experience in Islamabad. Known on foodpanda for its Tarragon Chicken Steak, Fettuccine Alfredo, Grilled Chicken Panini and refreshing Mint Margarita, Sierra blends cafe comfort with full-meal satisfaction.


With modern decor, soft seating, outdoor options and attentive staff, it is equally suited to casual coffee, family dinners and special evenings — including popular rooftop-style dining vibes Islamabad is famous for.
